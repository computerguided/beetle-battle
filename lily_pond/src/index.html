The pond

How to play

Take turns putting a frog on a lily pad that is empty or already yours.

Pads that touch are neighbours. A pad holds one frog fewer than it has neighbours — when it is full, every frog leaps to a different neighbouring pad.

Frogs landing on a pad win over whoever sits there, and may fill that pad in turn, setting off a chain of leaps.

The pond is yours when no frog of the other colour is left.

Hover a pad to see its neighbours. Restless frogs sit on a pad that is one frog from leaping.
